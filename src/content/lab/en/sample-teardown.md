## Question

Part boundaries and joining features can help explain an assembly sequence. Appearance alone cannot establish a material grade, internal dimension or reliability result. Record visible evidence first and keep inferences separate.

## Recording method

- Preserve orientation, part identifiers and connection relationships at each step.
- Distinguish threaded joints, snap fits, adhesive bonds and other retention methods.
- Record tools, required access and irreversible damage during removal.
- Mark processes and materials that observation cannot confirm as unverified.

## Evidence limits

Opening one sample can describe that sample, but cannot establish consistency across batches or long-term life. This entry is an example method and includes no actual sample photographs, measurements or teardown findings.
