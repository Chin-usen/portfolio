# Automotive camera lens page (L2)

Adds Chinese and English lessons for a generic six-element automotive camera lens module through the shared disassembly template. Sixteen semantic parts (barrel, six elements, spacers, stop, IR-cut filter, retaining ring, O-ring, holder, image sensor, PCB and active-alignment adhesive) carry bilingual name, role, features and placeholder material in Content Collections. A full-module view and an optical-train view share axial explosion: the lens stack leaves the barrel through its front, sub-assemblies separate along the optical axis, and canvas picks, part tags and stage labels share one selection.

A meridional ray-section card is rendered at build time as SVG from the same illustrative prescription (front glass sphere, mixed glass spheres and plastic aspheres, stop between L3 and L4, plate filter). Paraxial y-nu traces draw the 0°, 15° and 30° field bundles with distinct line styles and colours; the card is labelled "示意，不代表真实光学设计 / Illustrative, not a real optical design". Optical elements, stop, filter and sensor are keyboard-operable SVG buttons; selecting one highlights it in the model, the part tags, the section and the paraxial element table. The paraxial card lists f′, F-number, field angles, image heights, stop diameter, total track and back focal distance, with the trace equations and assumptions on the page.

The Three.js geometry is revolved from the lens sag profiles and the derived mechanical layout; the committed meshopt GLB and actual rendered WebP previews come from the same generator, and verification recomputes the paraxial metrics as well as the geometry and asset hashes. Touch devices keep the poster until "Load 3D model"; reduced-motion and low-resource modes never request Three or the GLB and keep part selection, the ray section and the data table. Page fonts are source-bound subsets under `/models/automotive-camera-lens/fonts/`. No shared component, other lab page, docs or version changes are required.
