# Pin-fin cold plate page (S3)

Adds Chinese and English S3 simulation pages at `/lab/pin-fin-cooler/` using the shared S1 simulation shell and surface styles. The page estimates the steady single-phase performance of a generic pin-fin cold plate from empirical correlations; no CFD is run.

- Heat transfer: Zukauskas tube-bank correlation (Incropera 6th ed., Eq. 7.58, Tables 7.5–7.6) with the row correction, including the single-cylinder approximation in the 10²–10³ band. Uniform pin-fin efficiency with adiabatic tips and the uncovered base area give the overall surface efficiency; effectiveness–NTU gives the base-to-inlet thermal resistance. Bulk and wall temperatures are iterated for the property and Pr_s evaluation.
- Pressure drop: Gaddis–Gnielinski bundle correlation (VDI Heat Atlas L1) with laminar/turbulent blending and the inlet/outlet term.
- Inputs: flow 2–20 L/min, inlet 25–85 °C, heat load, pin diameter 1–3 mm, transverse and longitudinal pitch 1.25–3 × D, pin height 4–10 mm, in-line/staggered, water or 50 % ethylene glycol, copper or aluminium pins, footprint (default 50 × 80 mm). Coolant properties are temperature-interpolated from tables generated with CoolProp 8.0.0 (IAPWS for water, Melinder 2010 for the glycol solution) by the authoring-only `model/coolant-table.py`.
- Outputs: R_th, Δp, mean h, Re_max and flow regime, calculation details and an R_th–Δp trade-off chart that sweeps the selected parameter, with a data table. Every correlation's Re, Pr, pitch-ratio, row-count, Biot, property-range and pin-aspect limits are listed with live status; out-of-range inputs are flagged in the headline, the sticky control summary and the validity card.
- Visuals: a parametric Three.js pin array rebuilt from the sliders (five named, pickable parts with bilingual Content Collection descriptions), a top-view streamline sketch labelled "示意，非 CFD / Illustrative, not CFD", and result cards. The repository script exports the default geometry as a meshopt GLB with WebP previews and records hashes in `public/models/pin-fin-cooler/model-manifest.json`.
- Loading: desktop pointers enhance to WebGL after paint through `scheduleVisibleEnhancement`; touch devices keep the static preview behind a "加载 3D 模型 / Load 3D model" button; reduced-motion and low-resource devices stay static with full calculations. Route exit disposes listeners, observers, geometry and the WebGL context (`pinFinLifecycle` diagnostics).
- Page-owned verified Noto subsets (four original weights) under `public/models/pin-fin-cooler/fonts/`.
- Unit tests compare the correlations with Incropera 6th ed. Examples 7.7 and 3.9 and Table A.6, and cover unit conversions and range boundaries; a browser spec covers interaction, warnings, static/touch/desktop loading, fonts and ten Home round trips.
